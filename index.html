Reconnect with yourself, and then with others.

We're now living in an age of anxiety.

Remember when you could share something without wondering how many people liked it? When a conversation was just a conversation — not a performance for an audience you never asked for?

Somewhere along the way, social media stopped being social. Algorithms decided what you saw. Ads learned what you feared. The scroll became endless by design — because stopping was never the point.

Legacy is something quieter. A place to know yourself, connect with the people who matter most, and have an Ally in your corner when you need one.

I
Built to Let Go

Most apps are designed to keep you inside them. Legacy is designed to send you back out — to the people and things that matter most to you.

II
Depth Over Engagement

No algorithms optimized for your attention. Every feature traces back to one question: does this bring you closer to yourself, and others?

III
Your Data, Encrypted

Journal entries, messages, and Ally insights are encrypted end-to-end. What you share with Legacy stays with you.
