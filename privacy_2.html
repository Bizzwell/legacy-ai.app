2. What We Collect

We collect only what's necessary to operate the app:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Journal entries — the text, photos, audio, and voice recordings you create. All personal journal content is encrypted on your device before being stored.
  • Social content — posts, comments, and interactions you share with your circles. When you choose to share something with your community, you are choosing to make it visible to others — social content is stored in a form that allows it to be displayed to the people you share it with.
  • Messages — direct messages between you and other users or your AI Allies, encrypted end-to-end.
  • Incognito / alias content — content created under an alias, stored in cryptographic isolation from your real identity. Alias bios and handles visible to other users are stored in displayable form.
  • AI profile data — themes, values, and patterns Legacy's AI infers from your journal entries over time, used to personalize your AI experience. Stored encrypted.
  • Voice recordings — when you use voice entry, your audio file is stored and encrypted. To generate a transcript, audio must be readable by Google Speech-to-Text, which reads directly from your storage — so the file is briefly stored without DEK encryption during the transcription window, then encrypted once transcription completes. Audio you attach to an entry without transcribing is encrypted immediately. Transcripts are also processed by Anthropic's API for cleanup and formatting. You may share voice recordings to your social circles within the app.
  • Feedback reports — if you submit a bug report or feedback through the app, we collect your display name, email, a description of the issue, your current screen, device model, OS version, app version, recent error logs, and a screenshot of the app at the time of submission. Screenshots may contain visible app content. Feedback data is stored unencrypted.
  • Crash and error reports — we use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and non-fatal errors, tagged with your user ID. This includes device state at the time of the crash. Crash reports are sent to Google.
  • Usage data — basic app usage counters (number of AI interactions, feature usage) to enforce tier limits and manage costs. Not used for advertising.
  • Device and session information — device type, OS version, timezone, and push notification token, for debugging, compatibility, and notification delivery.
  • Referral and invite codes — if you use a referral or invite code at signup, we store the code and the relationship between accounts.
  • Moderation reports — if you report another user's content, we store the report. Reports are retained even after account deletion, as moderation history.

3. What We Don't Collect

We do not collect advertising identifiers. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with data brokers. We do not track your location. We do not read your journal entries to target you with ads — ever.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for exactly one purpose: to operate Legacy for you.

  • Journal entries and messages are stored so you can access them.
  • AI profile data is used to personalize your Legacy AI experience — to make the AI more attuned to you over time.
  • Usage counters enforce subscription limits and help us manage infrastructure costs transparently.
  • Account information is used to identify you, send account-related emails, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Crash reports help us identify and fix bugs without requiring you to report them manually.

We do not use your data to train AI models. Your journal entries and messages are processed by Anthropic's API to generate responses. Under Anthropic's published commercial API terms, inputs and outputs from API usage are not used to train their models by default. See Anthropic's Privacy Center for current details.

5. Encryption

Your personal content — journal entries, messages, voice recordings, AI profile data — is encrypted before it reaches our servers. What you choose to share with your community is different: social posts, comments, and alias bios are stored in a form that allows them to be displayed to others, because that is what sharing means. The distinction is yours to make, and Legacy respects it.

  • Journal entries, AI profile data, and goals are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a Device Encryption Key (DEK) generated on your device and protected by your device's secure hardware (iOS Secure Enclave or Android Keystore). Your DEK never leaves your device unencrypted.
  • Direct messages between users are encrypted end-to-end using X25519 key agreement. Only the intended recipients can decrypt them.
  • Incognito / alias messages use separate per-alias keypairs, cryptographically isolating alias identity from real identity.
  • Recovery phrase — when you set up Legacy, you receive a 12-word BIP39 recovery phrase. This phrase is the only way to recover your encrypted data if you lose access to your device. We cannot recover your data without it. Store it somewhere safe.
Your journal entries, AI profile, and direct messages are not stored in a readable format — by us or anyone else. Content you share with Legacy AI passes through our servers in order to reach Anthropic's API, but is not persisted in plaintext. Firebase holds only encrypted copies of your encryption key and cannot decrypt your content. If you lose your recovery phrase and your device, your data is unrecoverable — by you or by us.

6. Third-Party Services

Legacy uses a small number of third-party services to operate. Each receives only the data necessary for its function:

  • Firebase (Google) — cloud database, authentication, push notifications, file storage, and crash reporting (Crashlytics). Your encrypted content is stored in Firebase Firestore and Storage. Firebase holds only encrypted copies of your encryption keys and cannot decrypt your personal content. Crash reports sent to Crashlytics include your user ID and device state. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — powers Legacy AI and your AI Allies. Journal entries, messages, and voice transcripts you share with the AI are sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Under Anthropic's published commercial API terms, this content is not stored in a readable format and is not used to train models by default. Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • Google Speech-to-Text — transcribes voice journal entries. Audio sent for transcription is briefly stored without DEK encryption while Google reads it directly from your storage bucket, then encrypted once transcription completes. Google's service credentials read the file directly — your audio never passes through Legacy's servers during transcription. Data logging is disabled at the GCP project level, meaning Google does not retain your audio for service improvement or training purposes. Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

We do not use advertising SDKs or data brokers. We do not integrate with social networks for tracking purposes.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account).

  • When you request deletion, your profile and feed posts are hidden immediately.
  • A 30-day grace period begins. You can reactivate your account by signing back in during this window.
  • After 30 days, your journal entries, messages, AI profile, goals, and media are permanently deleted from our servers. This cannot be undone.
  • What is retained after deletion: Direct messages you sent to other users are anonymized to "Legacy Echo" rather than deleted, to preserve the integrity of those conversations. Content you posted under aliases (forum posts, comments, chatroom messages) is anonymized to "Legacy Ghost" and retained. Your public encryption key is preserved so other users' encrypted messages to you remain readable by them. Moderation reports you submitted or that were filed against you are retained as moderation history.
  • Encrypted database backups are retained for 7 days (Firestore), 30 days (auth), and up to 90 days (pre-restore snapshots), after which they are permanently deleted.

We do not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months.

10. Security

Security is structural, not aspirational. In addition to the device-level encryption described in Section 5, we use Firebase Security Rules to restrict data access, HTTPS for all data transmission, and ongoing security reviews. We conducted a formal security audit in July 2026 covering our encryption implementation and conduct periodic reviews as the app evolves.

No system is completely immune to breaches. If a security incident affects your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.
